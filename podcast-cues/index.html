Running order

Times are minutes from "Hi, and welcome". Tap a row to look at it; in a live session, "Back to live" returns you. Your questions are yellow; your handovers are purple.

    2:45 pm, before Nathan joins

      House rules

      Stay muted

      Unmute only when the answer before your question is wrapping up. Mute again as soon as Nathan starts answering.

      Wait two seconds

      When Nathan stops talking, count two seconds before you speak. No talking over him, and the editor gets a clean cut.

      React short, then ask

      One short reaction in your own words, then read your question exactly as written. Then stop talking.

      Don't explain the question

      No "so my question is basically…". The question is short on purpose.

      Nod, don't "mm-hmm"

      While Nathan talks, react with your face, not your voice. Background "yeah" ruins clips.

      Hand over in one line

      When the next question belongs to someone else, the person who asked last hands over: "Quan, over to you." The page shows you the exact line.

      Look at the camera

      Especially while you ask. Light in front of you, not behind.

      Never share your screen

      This page is for us. Nathan should never see it.

      If something goes wrong

      Someone can't make it

      Aimum marks them off the call. Their questions move to the right person automatically and the handovers update.

      Someone drops mid-call

      Message OUT on WhatsApp. Aimum marks you off the call and your next question moves on everyone's screen.

      Nathan already covers a later topic

      If he answers a later question on his own while telling another story, Aimum skips that question.

      Running behind

      Cut in this order: 5a, 5b, Q6, 3a, 1a. Never cut the intro, the PinnTag question, Q1 to Q5, Q7 or the Closing.

      Aimum drops

      Quan hosts. Read Aimum's lines from your screen until he's back. The Zoom recording keeps running.

      Nathan drops

      Stay on, stay muted, don't chat about it on the call. Aimum messages Nathan. We cut the gap later.

      Nathan goes quiet

      He may be thinking. Give him 3 to 4 seconds. If he looks unsure what we mean, Aimum asks it again more simply.

      You fluff your question

      Stop, say "Let me ask that again", and repeat it cleanly. The editor cuts the first try.

      WhatsApp signals

      Use the team WhatsApp group during the call, never the Zoom chat: Nathan can see it and it is saved with the recording.
